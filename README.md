# Muhammed Mirshad - Portfolio & Bio-Link Hub 🚀

A modern, high-performance, mobile-optimized personal portfolio and digital bio-link hub for **Muhammed Mirshad** — Managing Director at **KKL**, Founder of **KAE Production**, **KAE Advertise**, and **Foreignbites India**.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

- **Neo-Dark Glassmorphism UI**: High-end aesthetic with ambient glow gradients, frosted glass panels, portrait photo showcase, and modern typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans` & `Space Grotesk`).
- **Interactive Role Typewriter**: Cycles dynamically through leadership roles, video production, growth marketing, and engineering student status.
- **Bento Grid Architecture**:
  - 💼 **Ventures & Brands**: Cards for KKL (MD), KAE Production, KAE Advertise, and Foreignbites India with direct Instagram links.
  - ⚡ **Core Specializations**: Video Editing (Cinematic, Reels, Color Grading), Digital Marketing (Growth, Strategy, Analytics), and EEE Engineering.
  - 🎓 **Education & Background**: Ma'din College of Engineering & Management focus and stats.
  - 📬 **Direct Contact Grid**: 1-click email copy, direct phone call, and 1-click WhatsApp messaging (`+91 7510713036` & `+91 7510910260`).
  - 🌐 **Social Presence**: Direct links to LinkedIn, Instagram (@mirshadkk_), and Snapchat.
- **📱 Instant vCard / .vcf Generator**: Visitors can tap "Save to Contacts" to instantly download `Muhammed_Mirshad.vcf` and add your details straight to their phone's contacts.
- **🔲 QR Code Modal**: Tap the QR icon to display a scan-ready QR code for instant in-person networking.
- **🔗 Native Web Share**: Instant mobile sharing menu support with automatic clipboard fallback.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply double-click `index.html` or open it with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

Or, if you have Python available:
```powershell
cd f:\mirshad-portfolio
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

## 🌐 Free 1-Click Hosting Options

You can host this website online for free with your own custom domain or free subdomain:

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Free)
1. Create a GitHub repository (e.g. `mirshad-portfolio`).
2. Upload the files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `assets/profile.jpg`).
3. Go to **Settings > Pages** and select `main` branch -> Save.
4. Your site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/mirshad-portfolio/`.

### Option 2: Vercel or Netlify (Drag & Drop)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) or [netlify.com](https://netlify.com).
2. Drag and drop the `f:\mirshad-portfolio` folder.
3. Your site goes live instantly with a free SSL certificate.
